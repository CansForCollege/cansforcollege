The Process

How It Works

Our student associates manage every step — from signing up households to collecting cans and tracking environmental impact in real time.

1
🏠

Community Sign-Up

Households in your neighborhood register to participate. Each member receives a unique ID and a scheduled weekly pickup day.

2
🥫

Weekly Collection

Student associates visit each home on their assigned day, collect aluminum cans, and log the weight at every pickup.

3
📊

Impact Tracking

Every pound is recorded. Associates can view totals per household, track routes on an interactive map, and monitor program-wide impact.

4
🎓

Scholarships Funded

Recycling proceeds go directly toward college scholarships for participating student associates — turning community service into opportunity.

♻️

Every aluminum can recycled saves enough energy to power a TV for three hours. Together, we're making a measurable difference.

About the Initiative

More Than Recycling

Cans for College was created to give high school students a meaningful way to serve their community while building toward their future. Associates gain real-world experience in logistics, data tracking, and community outreach.

🌱
Environmental Impact Every pound of aluminum recycled reduces landfill waste and saves energy — tracked and measured in real time.
🤝
Community Building Neighbors participate, students engage, and the entire community becomes part of something bigger.
🎓
Scholarship Funding Recycling revenue is converted into college scholarship funds for student associates who lead the program.
📱
Tech-Enabled Tracking Associates use our digital management platform to register households, log collections, and visualize routes on a live map.
Why It Matters

Triple Impact

Cans for College delivers on three fronts simultaneously — for students, for the planet, and for the community.

🌍

For the Planet

Aluminum is infinitely recyclable. Every can we collect stays out of a landfill and back in the production cycle, reducing carbon emissions and conserving natural resources.

🎓

For Students

Associates earn scholarship funding while developing leadership, communication, and data management skills that prepare them for college and careers.

🏘️

For the Community

Participating households make recycling effortless. The program builds pride, strengthens neighborhood connections, and creates a culture of sustainability.
